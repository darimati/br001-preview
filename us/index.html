The Seoul Last

Built for a wider, higher foot

DARIMATI is a Seoul company. BR-001 is built on a last shaped to the average Korean foot — measurably broader across the forefoot and higher over the instep than the Western lasts most trainers use.

If you size up in other brands just to get width, you don't need to here. And if you've never found a trainer that stops pinching at the widest part of your foot, this is the reason to try one.

Stable Core™

Cushion isn't the problem.
Platform width is.

"Won't a cushioned shoe feel tippy under a bar?" It's a fair worry, and usually blamed on the wrong thing.

A running midsole is drawn narrow and round because that's what rolls forward efficiently. Load it sideways under a bar and that shape gives first. Stable Core™ keeps the stack and spreads the base, so the contact patch stays flat when the load goes lateral.

Measure your foot

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall.
  2. Mark the paper at your longest toe.
  3. Measure wall to mark. Use the longer foot.
  4. Measure in the evening — feet are at their largest then.

Confirm — US M/W equivalents are the standard mondopoint conversion. Check them against a real fitting before launch, and decide whether to label the product in US sizes or in mm.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

Will it feel tippy under a squat?

That feeling usually comes from a narrow platform, not from cushioning. Stable Core™ keeps the stack height and widens the base so the contact patch stays flat when load goes sideways. See the cross-section above.

Is it as solid as a dedicated lifting shoe?

No. A flat, rigid, raised-heel lifter is more solid under maximal loads, and if heavy squatting is most of what you do, that's the better tool. BR-001 is built for a session that mixes conditioning and barbell work.

Can I actually run in it?

Yes. The midsole is 100% A-TPU, the compound used in premium running models, and it returns energy on landing. It's built for warm-ups, conditioning and everyday miles.

I have wide feet. Will these fit?

This is the shoe's main point of difference. The last is shaped to the average Korean foot — wider across the forefoot and higher over the instep than most Western lasts. If you normally size up for width, don't here; take your measured size.

Do you offer 2E or 4E widths?

Not as separate widths. The standard last is already built wide, which is why there's one width rather than several.

What if the size is wrong?

Send them back within 30 days. See returns below.
